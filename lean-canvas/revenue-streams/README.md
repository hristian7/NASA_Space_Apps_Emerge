# Revenue streams and market size

Updated 18 Nov 2026. **All prices and buyer counts are `[U]` estimates unless tagged.** The counts and their sources are in [`../evidence.csv`](../evidence.csv).

## How we charge (hypotheses)

| Segment | Model | Price `[U]` | Anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Electric utilities | Annual licence per service territory: daily risk, alerts, fire history | €50–250k/year | Pano AI charges about $50k per camera a year `[D]`; Technosylva sells enterprise contracts (no public price) |
| Insurers, reinsurers, specialist insurers | Annual licence or per-location scoring | from €100k/year | None found |
| Governments | Regional dashboard licence. Free for firefighters | €20–100k per region/year | Greek, Spanish and US government fire-tech spending `[D]` |
| Forest-carbon projects | Per-forest risk report and monitoring | €5–20k per forest/year | None found; forest data sells at $0.10–0.25/ha `[D]` |

## Market size: three layers

- **Total spending on wildfire (TAM):** billions a year. California's investor-owned utilities alone spend about $9bn a year on wildfire mitigation (projected to 2028) `[V]`. Our product could take only a slice of it.
- **Reachable market (SAM):** about **€54M a year** `[U]`, fire-prone regions only:

| Buyers | Count | × price | = per year |
|---|---|---|---|
| Utilities: 170 US utilities with wildfire plans `[D]` (PNNL database), about 60 in Southern Europe, about 14 in Australia, about 15 in Canada and Chile | about 260 | €100k | about €26M |
| Insurers and reinsurers: about 100 US, 50 EU, 30 reinsurers | about 180 | €100k | about €18M |
| Government regions: about 60 Mediterranean EU regions (NUTS-2), 15 US states, 8 Australian states | about 80 | €60k | about €5M |
| Forest-carbon projects (Verra and California compliance) | about 500 | €10k | about €5M |

- **3-year goal (SOM):** about €1.8M a year `[U]`, around 3% of SAM:
  - 8 utilities;
  - 4 insurers;
  - 5 government regions;
  - about 40 carbon projects.
- **Growth beyond SAM:**
  - bigger contracts with large utilities;
  - new buyers: rail, telecom, agriculture, real estate;
  - more regions as fires spread into Central and Northern Europe.

**Why this matters:** YC-style investors look for markets that can reach $1bn or more, so on its own a €54M SAM looks small. Show the growth path.
