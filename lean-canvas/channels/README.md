# Channels

Updated 18 Nov 2026. **Not researched or tested yet.** Everything here is a hypothesis `[U]`.

| Channel | Segment | Why we think it works |
|---|---|---|
| Direct sales to utility wildfire-mitigation teams | Utilities | They make daily fire decisions and already buy forecasts ([customer segments](../customer-segments/README.md)) |
| Insurance data marketplaces and platforms (e.g. Verisk Model Exchange, Addresscloud) | Insurers | Skyblu reaches insurers through Addresscloud `[D]` |
| Reinsurers and brokers (e.g. Guy Carpenter, Howden Re) | Insurers | They use hazard data in submissions and track wildfire accumulation `[D]` |
| Public tenders | Governments | Greece, the Canary Islands and Murcia buy fire tech through tenders `[D]` |
| The free public app (emergefire.app) | All | A free tier for firefighters, and a demo for buyers |
| Partnerships, e.g. with OroraTech (they detect, we predict) | All | They own the satellites; we add the forecast |

**First step:** 20 interviews with utilities, insurers and fire services in Spain, Portugal, Greece and Australia, aiming for 3 paid pilots.
