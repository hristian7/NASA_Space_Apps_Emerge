# Unfair advantage

Updated 18 Nov 2026. **Honest answer: none proven yet.**

## What is *not* an advantage

- **The data.** NASA, NOAA and Copernicus data are free to everyone. Technosylva or OroraTech could rebuild our method quickly.
- **Covering Europe.** Technosylva started in Spain and serves Spanish regions; Skyblu covers Europe for insurers ([competition](../competition/README.md)).
- **Publishing our accuracy.** It builds trust, but the owner judged it weak as a differentiator.

## What could become one

1. **Cost and speed:** a small AI-native team on free data can sell at a fraction of incumbents' prices, and reach buyers they find too small (co-ops, small utilities, small regions, carbon projects) `[U]`.
2. **A record of daily, publicly scored forecasts:** it grows every day and can't be bought later `[U]`.
3. **Customers' own data:** fire and outage records from customers would improve the model for everyone, which is hard to copy `[U]`.
4. **Workflow lock-in:** alerts and data feeds wired into customers' daily tools `[U]`.
