# Problem

Updated 18 Nov 2026. Numbers and links: [`../evidence.csv`](../evidence.csv).

## Three problems

1. **Nobody can see where fire will start tomorrow.** Fire services decide one day ahead where to send crews, but they don't know which forecasts are accurate.
2. **The satellite fire record is split.** NASA's MODIS (since 2000) and VIIRS (since 2012) can't be compared directly: VIIRS sees about 2.7× more fire `[V]` ([`../../docs/research/first-run-bulgaria-usa.md`](../../docs/research/first-run-bulgaria-usa.md)). This is the NASA challenge itself ([`../../docs/challenge-brief.md`](../../docs/challenge-brief.md)).
3. **The data is scattered.** Crews check about 10 websites (EFFIS, NASA FIRMS, weather forecasts).

## Evidence from users

One interview so far, with a Bulgarian firefighter of about 20 years' service (14 Nov 2026). Notes: [`../../docs/research/firefighter-interview.md`](../../docs/research/firefighter-interview.md). His figures are `[U]`; the service length comes from the team member who held the interview.

- **About 10 websites:** crews open about 10 sites to plan a day.
- **Top need:** an accurate forecast of where fire will start the next day, so a team can wait there (prevention and preparedness).
- **Accuracy:** firefighters have no idea how accurate current forecasts are.
- **Money:** fire services don't pay for tools, because the EU's EFFIS is free. This shaped the business model: firefighters get a free tier, and others pay ([`../customer-segments/README.md`](../customer-segments/README.md)).

**Gap:** one interview is not validation. Next: 20 interviews with paying segments.

## Why now

- **2025 was the EU's worst fire season on record:** 1,079,538 ha burned `[V]` (JRC).
- **July 2026, France and Spain:** over 300,000 people displaced `[V]` (AP). The firefighter's "400,000" matches no source, so don't use it.
- **USA, 2026 to 13 Nov:** 9.48M acres burned, about 1.9× the same date in 2025 `[V]` (NIFC). This is the 5th-worst year since 1983, not the worst; the record is 10.13M acres in 2015 `[V]`.
- **Los Angeles, January 2025:**
  - 31 deaths `[V]`: AP via PBS, Jan 2026; an April 2025 count gave 30.
  - About $40B insured, $53B total `[V]` (Munich Re).
- **One fire can sink a utility:** PG&E went bankrupt in 2019 facing about $30B in wildfire claims `[D]`. California's investor-owned utilities spent over $40B on wildfire mitigation in 2019–24 `[V]`.
- **Worldwide:**
  - Extreme wildfires became about 2.2× more frequent over 2003–2023 `[D]` (Nature Ecology & Evolution 2024).
  - Total burned area worldwide has **fallen**, mainly as savanna turns to farmland `[D]`.
  - So say "extreme fires are growing", not "fires are growing everywhere".

## What not to say

- **"The US had its worst year on record":** wrong; it's the 5th worst.
- **"Fires are growing everywhere in the world":** total burned area is down.
- **"400,000 evacuated in France and Spain":** no source; use "over 300,000".
