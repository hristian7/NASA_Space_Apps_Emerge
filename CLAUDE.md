# Instructions for AI agents in this project

Read [`README.md`](README.md) first.

- **This repo becomes public and open source** (NASA Space Apps Terms). Put **no personal information** in it: no names beyond the public team page, no contacts, no career notes, no private-repo details. **No keys or tokens:** the FIRMS MAP_KEY goes in a git-ignored `.env`.
- **Tag every number:** `[V]` verified at a primary source · `[D]` directional · `[U]` our inference.
- **Never let the AI be its own oracle.** Results are checked against real data, an independent sensor or published numbers ([`docs/verification-plan.md`](docs/verification-plan.md)). Expected answers are written before the code, by someone other than the code's author.
- **Answers to the team:** short, plain, skimmable. Use summary tables, and explain technical terms.
- **Hard rules:** no work on the challenge before Sat 14 Nov 09:00. List every external resource for the References field. Track how AI was used, for the SI usage note.
